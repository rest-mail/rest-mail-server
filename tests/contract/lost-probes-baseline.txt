# probes remain selected. A new operation or a larger count fails. A smaller count
# is noted by the gate: lower it here in the same change once a topology run shows it.
# Measured with a whole-API run of Vertrag 0.42.0 against a local build, 2026-11-03,

# 107 across 10 after the pins and setup of Part of #1443. A probe counted here was
# not sent, so it proves nothing.
# The local dependency and skip differences do not prove the gate.
